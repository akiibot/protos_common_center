# Product Vision

## Company context

Protos is a bootstrapped, seven-person AI-powered web development company based in Dhaka. Its long-term path is:

1. establish an AI-powered service business;
2. generate sustainable cash flow;
3. build a recognizable public brand;
4. launch SaaS products; and
5. provide specialized AI agents to Bangladeshi businesses.

The team combines business development, product management, web development, AI/ML, UI/UX, graphics, video, photography, illustration, operations, and logistics.

## Product purpose

Common Center should give the team one reliable place to answer five questions:

1. What are we trying to achieve?
2. What needs attention now?
3. Who owns the next action?
4. What is the commercial and delivery status?
5. What did we decide, change, or learn?

It replaces fragmented spreadsheet coordination with connected records and explicit ownership. It is an internal operating product, not a generic project-management clone.

## Primary users

- **Founder:** company direction, sales, commercial decisions, AI integration, product management.
- **Operations lead:** planning, coordination, deadlines, risks, and reporting.
- **Delivery team:** projects, tasks, milestones, blockers, and reusable knowledge.
- **Content team:** ideas, briefs, production, review, scheduling, and results.
- **Finance owner:** invoices, cash received, expenses, and project profitability.

One person may hold several of these responsibilities during the company's foundation stage.

## Product principles

### One source of operational truth

Data should be entered once and reused. A won lead should become a client and project without recreating the same information.

### Attention before reporting

The first screen should identify decisions, risks, overdue actions, and the next commitments—not merely show charts.

### Accountability without surveillance

Work needs one accountable owner and a visible status. The system should support coordination without measuring people through invasive activity tracking.

### Evidence behind every metric

Dashboard numbers must trace back to records. Revenue must distinguish pipeline, committed work, invoices, and received cash.

### Bangladesh-first operation

Use Asia/Dhaka dates, BDT financial reporting, mobile-friendly interactions, and workflows appropriate for local SMB sales and delivery.

### Safe, reversible automation

Automation should surface or prepare actions before making high-impact changes. Every automated mutation must be auditable.

## Core domains

| Domain | Primary outcome |
|---|---|
| Strategy | Goals connect to measurable work and results. |
| Sales | Every opportunity has an owner, next action, date, and value. |
| Clients | Commercial, communication, and delivery context stay connected. |
| Projects | Scope, milestones, tasks, risks, files, and money remain visible. |
| Tasks | Each commitment has one owner and one current state. |
| Content | Company work becomes a consistent public narrative. |
| Finance | The team understands commitments, invoices, cash, cost, and margin. |
| Team | Responsibilities and capacity are clear. |
| Knowledge | Decisions, playbooks, and templates stay close to the work. |

## Success measures

- The team stops maintaining duplicate operational spreadsheets.
- Every active lead and project has a current next action.
- Blockers and overdue work are visible before weekly meetings.
- Weekly operating reporting takes minutes rather than hours.
- Revenue and cash figures reconcile with the underlying records.
- New collaborators can understand the company workflow from the system.

## Explicit non-goals for the next release

- replacing accounting software;
- public client self-service portals;
- employee surveillance or productivity scoring;
- building every possible project-management feature;
- autonomous AI changes without approval; and
- multi-company SaaS architecture before Protos proves its own workflow.
